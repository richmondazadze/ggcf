02 / ACCESS & MOTION
Clear in every state.
Visible focus; 44px minimum targets; persistent labels; errors with recovery instructions. Never rely on color alone.
150–220ms transitions. Respect reduced motion. Avoid autoplay carousels and decorative delays.
