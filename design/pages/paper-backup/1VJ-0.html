Build notes — outside the website copy
Typography: Clash Display Semibold 600 for the wordmark. Switzer Medium 500 for headlines and buttons. Switzer Regular 400 for body copy.
Navigation: fixed after 80px scroll; 72px desktop / 72px mobile. Active link uses green with a short underline. Mobile menu traps focus and closes with Escape.
Motion: hero 700ms / 20px travel; section reveal 650ms / 22px. Desktop photographs have a maximum 34px parallax range. Hover cards tilt no more than 1.5°. Button feedback 200ms.
Reduced motion: static photographs and cards; immediate content. Mobile uses natural scrolling without parallax or perspective. Carousel is manual and retains both original slides.
Forms: persistent labels, 52px controls, 44px minimum targets, visible 3px focus ring. Required, error, loading and success behavior needs the existing approved copy before implementation.
Payments: keep PayPal, Mobile Money and international transfer details. Monthly giving is a visual state until recurring payments are implemented. No invented prices or donation outcomes.
Content: every original heading, paragraph, team quote, statistic and action is retained. Global statistics display their existing final counter values. Photo crops use the foundation’s real imagery.
