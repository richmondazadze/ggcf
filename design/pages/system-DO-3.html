Who We Are
Good God Charity Foundation
Good God Charity Foundation is a Non-profit Charitable Foundation founded in 2022 with a dedication to improving child health and education in Ghana and beyond. Through healthcare programs, educational support, and community outreach, we aim to raise awareness, increase donations, and foster community involvement in underrepresented areas.
