Our Impact

December 2024

600+ students supported with educational materials

600+ students were supported with educational materials. In December 2024, Good God Charity Foundation visited New Amakom Primary School. The outreach brought volunteers and pupils together for a donation event centred on practical support and personal connection.

Meeting pupils where they learn

The photographs place this story at the school itself. Pupils gather around the volunteers as items are shared, while other pictures capture groups of children in front of the New Amakom school building. The visit brought the foundation’s work into the setting where the pupils spend their school day.

The donation was also an opportunity to spend time with the children. The images record conversation, attention, and moments shared between pupils and volunteers. That personal connection is part of GGCF’s approach to giving: responding to practical needs while showing care for the people behind them.

Another step in supporting education

The school visit added an education-focused chapter to the foundation’s earlier orphanage and healthcare donations. It reflected the same commitment to bringing support to children in the places that care for and educate them.

The photographs preserve the people and setting of that day. Together, they tell the story of a school community visited, pupils engaged, and support shared.
