# GGCF sharing image

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, using the authentic GGCF logo and New Amakom outreach photograph as references. Exported to exactly 1200 × 630 pixels in JPEG (sharing metadata) and PNG (downloadable master).

Art direction: premium editorial charity card; deep teal, ivory and orange; real schoolchildren at right; authentic logo and geometric GOOD GOD wordmark at upper left; large existing message “Giving hope to those who need it the most!” and supporting line “Child Health and Education”. No donation statistics, buttons or added claims. A targeted second generation refined only the wordmark toward Clash Display Semibold and the foundation line toward Switzer Medium, retaining the composition.

Assets: `preview/assets/og-ggcf.jpg` and `preview/assets/og-ggcf.png`.

The page generator adds Open Graph and Twitter large-image metadata to all preview pages. `GGCF_SHARE_ORIGIN` can override the branch-preview origin when preparing production. Vercel preview authentication still applies: public social crawlers cannot fetch a protected preview. Production remains unchanged until the approved replacement.
