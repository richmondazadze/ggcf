Christmas Support at Missionaries of Charity Sisters

60 children with special needs were supported through this visit. In December 2025, Good God Charity Foundation brought its giving campaign to Missionaries of Charity Sisters in Ashtown-Mbrom, with a focus on supporting children with special needs.

Coming together to give

The campaign invited supporters to join a donation visit on 20 December 2025. Its focus was children with special needs in the care of Missionaries of Charity Sisters. The invitation placed practical generosity at the centre of the Christmas season: coming together to support children and the people caring for them.

The event photographs show GGCF volunteers arranging a broad collection of supplies. Bottled water and drinks, packaged food, tissue products, bedding, and other bundled items are visible around the donation table. Each part of the collection reflects the same purpose: bringing useful support to the home.

Practical care, shared with love

The visit continued GGCF’s commitment to supporting vulnerable children through direct action. At the centre of the story are the children, their carers, and a group of people willing to contribute what they could.

This Christmas outreach forms another chapter in the foundation’s work: turning a public invitation to give into supplies brought to the people the campaign was created to support.
